# Community conduct

Everyone participating in Xzare issues, discussions, reviews and contributions should treat others respectfully, regardless of background or experience. Arabic and English are both welcome.

Give specific, constructive feedback on work. Respect privacy, attribution and different levels of technical familiarity. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, spam and disclosure of someone else's sensitive data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to **hello@xzare.com**. Include links and context where possible. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will be handled with care, but no absolute confidentiality or response-time guarantee is offered.

This policy applies to project-managed collaboration spaces and people representing the project there. It does not prevent good-faith criticism of technical decisions.
