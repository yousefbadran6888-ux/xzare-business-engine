Double-entry journals: drafts editable with optimistic version, balanced positive debit/credit lines, active leaf accounts, strict dates/period membership. Manager posts immutable journals; correction by linked reversing entry once, original intact. Clerk prepares drafts but cannot post. Closed periods block posting/editing and reversals into them; admin closes after all drafts resolved and trial balance reconciled.
Reports from posted ledger only: chronological running ledger including opening balance, trial balance opening/movement/ending debit-credit, period income, balance sheet including accumulated net income. Verify A=L+E, journal balance and ledger headers against line sums. Safe integer cents, explicit date filters, no financial-statement claims across unreconciled ledgers. CSV neutralizes formula prefixes, print/PDF through browser print.
